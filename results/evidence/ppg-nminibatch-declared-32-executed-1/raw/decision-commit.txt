ppg nminibatch: declare 1, and make an inexpressible config fatal

card0-20260909-115331 ran 600,000 frames declaring --nminibatch 32 and executing 1, logging
"Warning: nminibatch > ntrain!! (32 > 1)" 293 times. minibatch_optimize splits the LEADING axis and
Roller.singles_to_multi documents that axis as "(batch, time)", so ntrain is num_envs and any
nminibatch above 1 is inexpressible at num_envs=1. Verified from the producer rather than from the
earlier note.

The resolution inverts the earlier conclusion. Against PPG's own released 1-rank recipe -- 64x256 =
16384 with 1 epoch x 8 minibatches, 8 gradient steps of 2048 samples, 0.00048828 steps per env frame
-- our executed 1 step of 2048 samples per 2048 frames is 0.00048828 per env frame. Density AND
per-step batch size are identical. The clamp did not damage fidelity to PPG; the declared 32 would
have, at 32x upstream density on 64-sample steps. So the defect was the declaration, and the run
itself is a faithful PPG needing no re-run.

raileanu21a-supp.pdf SS E's "2048 steps, 1 process" and its "32 minibatches" are jointly
inexpressible in PPG's released code, because that grid was searched in a codebase which flattens
process x steps before splitting while PPG's splits the environment axis alone. source_target for
this family is PPG's released code, so where both cannot hold, the code's own budget is what we
match. Raising num_envs to 32 would have made 32 expressible via a 32x64 rollout that appears in
neither source -- trading a false declaration for an unsourced one.

clipfrac 0.000 and approxkl 1e-13 are consequently correct rather than vacuous: with one minibatch
per rollout every update is exactly on-policy, so the ratio is 1 by construction. PPG's own 8
minibatches drift off-policy within a rollout, which is what makes its clipfrac non-zero.

The clamp now raises. A configuration that cannot execute must not be recordable as though it had,
and a future num_envs change that invalidates the declared value fails at the first optimisation
call rather than 293 warnings deep. A test pins families.json's declaration against the guard, so
the two cannot drift.

Swept every vendored trainer for the same warn-and-substitute shape: this was the only declared
hyperparameter silently adjusted at runtime.
